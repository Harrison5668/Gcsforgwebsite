About Us 关于我们

About Global CSR Foundation 关于全球企业社会责任基金会

The Global CSR Foundation (GCSRF) is a U.S.-registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, dedicated to empowering corporations, organizations, and individuals to participate in the United Nations' 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. Recognizing the urgency and severity of the challenges faced by humanity, GCSRF acts as a strong voice and a partner in raising awareness, sharing best practices, and inspiring participation to achieve real change.

全球企业社会责任基金会（Global CSR Foundation，简称 GCSRF）是一家在美国注册的 501(c)(3) 非营利组织，致力于赋能企业、机构和个人，积极参与联合国《2030 年可持续发展议程》。鉴于人类面临挑战的紧迫性与严峻性，GCSRF 致力于成为有力的倡导者与合作伙伴，通过提升公众认知、分享最佳实践经验、激发广泛参与，推动实现真正的变革。

We are engaged in three core areas,

我们主要专注于三个核心领域：

  • education and training programs on CSR and SDGs,
  • 企业社会责任（CSR）与可持续发展目标（SDGs）的教育与培训项目；
  • strategic planning and execution of customized CSR+SDG programs, as well as
  • 定制化 CSR + SDG 项目的战略规划与执行；
  • impact investing and social entrepreneurship in line with the UN's SDGs.
  • 与联合国可持续发展目标相契合的影响力投资与社会创业。

GCSRF's Board, Executive Committees and think tank advisors are comprised of political and business leaders and seasoned practitioners.

GCSRF 的董事会、执行委员会以及智库顾问团队由政界与商界领袖及资深实践专家组成。

Background 背景

In 2012, the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) were born at the United Nations Conference on Sustainable Development in Rio de Janeiro. The objective was to produce a set of universal goals that met the urgent environmental, political and economic challenges facing our world.

2012 年，在巴西里约热内卢召开的联合国可持续发展大会上，可持续发展目标（SDGs）正式诞生。其目标是制定一套具有普遍适用性的目标，以应对当今世界所面临的紧迫环境、政治与经济挑战。

The SDGs are unique in that they cover issues that affect everyone. They reaffirm an international commitment to end poverty, permanently, everywhere. They are ambitious in making sure no one is left behind. More importantly, they involve us all to build a more sustainable, safer, more prosperous planet for all humanity.

SDGs 的独特之处在于其涵盖影响所有人的议题。它们重申了国际社会在全球范围内、永久性消除贫困的承诺。这一目标雄心勃勃，强调"一个都不能少"。更重要的是，它号召我们每一个人共同努力，建设一个更加可持续、更安全、更繁荣的地球家园。

In 2015, in a commitment letter to the U.N., thirty-two businesses from around the world pledged to work for the realization of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, a global development agenda adopted by 193 UN member states, guided by the SDGs.

2015 年，在致联合国的一封承诺信中，来自全球的 32 家企业承诺为实现《2030 年可持续发展议程》而努力。该议程由 193 个联合国会员国共同通过，并以可持续发展目标为指导。

Known as Agenda 2030, the document embodies a transformative vision for the planet's future, targeting poverty and hunger eradication, health and education improvement, urban sustainability, climate change combat, and the protection of oceans and forests.

该文件被称为"2030 议程"（Agenda 2030），它为地球未来描绘了一幅具有变革意义的蓝图，目标包括消除贫困与饥饿、改善健康与教育、推动城市可持续发展、应对气候变化，以及保护海洋与森林资源。

This was the first time businesses were "explicitly committing themselves to achieving the 2030 Agenda," said Haitian Lu, Chief of the Office of the Under Secretary General for Economic and Social Affairs.

联合国经济和社会事务副秘书长办公室主任 Haitian Lu 表示，这是企业首次"明确承诺致力于实现 2030 年议程"。

About CSW70 关于 CSW70

Economic independence is the foundation of women's dignity, safety, and agency. At the 70th Session of the Commission on the Status of Women (CSW70), the Global Women Leadership Summit will bring together global leaders, policymakers, entrepreneurs, investors, and youth champions to accelerate economic justice and strengthen women's leadership in the future economy. Grounded in CSW70's priority theme of access to justice and its review theme on women's participation in public life, the Summit highlights economic empowerment as both the pathway to and the proof of true gender equality.

经济独立是女性尊严、安全与自主权的基础。在联合国妇女地位委员会第70届会议（CSW70）期间，全球女性领导力峰会将汇聚来自全球的领导人、政策制定者、企业家、投资人及青年领袖，共同推动经济正义，加速女性在未来经济中的领导地位。本次峰会立足于 CSW70 的优先主题"司法可及性"，以及审议主题"女性参与公共生活"，强调经济赋权既是实现性别平等的路径，也是性别平等最有力的证明。
